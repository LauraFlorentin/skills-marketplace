# Project Initiation

Establish shared intent and authority before detailed planning. Do not invent sponsor approval, budget, dates, owners, or stakeholder commitments.

## Route

- **Charter:** purpose, problem, measurable outcomes, scope, non-scope, deliverables, constraints, assumptions, milestones, budget basis, governance, risks, and approval.
- **Stakeholder map:** role, interest, influence, impact, concerns, decision rights, engagement, owner, and evidence; do not infer sensitive traits or political views.
- **Kickoff:** objectives, pre-reads, agenda, decisions, roles, working agreements, risks, actions, and communication channels.

Read [initiation-reference.md](references/initiation-reference.md) only when detailed templates and examples are needed.

## Workflow

1. Identify the triggering decision, sponsor, project type, and required depth.
2. Gather existing business case, requirements, constraints, prior decisions, and policies.
3. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and open decisions.
4. Draft the minimum initiation artifact that creates clarity.
5. Reconcile scope, outcomes, authority, resources, risks, and timeline.
6. Assign approval and follow-up owners; record unresolved items.

## Quality checks

- Outcomes are measurable and distinct from outputs.
- In-scope and out-of-scope boundaries are explicit.
- Decision and escalation rights name accountable roles.
- Stakeholder engagement respects privacy, accessibility, and organizational policy.
- Dates and budgets show their source or uncertainty.
- Approval status is factual, not implied.

Return the artifact, assumption and decision log, approvals required, and next planning action.
