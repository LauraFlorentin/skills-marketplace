# Project Planning

Translate approved outcomes and scope into an executable, updateable plan. Avoid false precision: estimates, availability, dependencies, and risk scores must show their evidence and uncertainty.

## Route

- **WBS:** decompose deliverables into mutually understandable work packages with completion criteria and ownership.
- **Schedule:** sequence activities, dependencies, milestones, calendars, constraints, estimates, float, and critical path.
- **Resource plan:** roles, skills, capacity, allocation, availability, handoffs, and gaps; confirm with accountable managers.
- **Risk register:** cause, event, impact, likelihood, severity method, owner, response, trigger, contingency, and review date.
- **Communication plan:** audience, information need, channel, cadence, owner, feedback path, accessibility, and confidentiality.

Read [planning-reference.md](references/planning-reference.md) only for detailed methods, templates, formulas, and examples.

## Workflow

1. Reconcile the charter, deliverables, constraints, governance, and planning horizon.
2. Choose predictive, agile, or hybrid planning based on uncertainty and delivery model.
3. Build from deliverables to work, then dependencies, resources, risks, and communications.
4. Validate estimates and availability with the people accountable for the work.
5. Add baselines or commitments only after approval.
6. Define update cadence, variance thresholds, and change control.

## Quality checks

- Every work package supports an approved deliverable.
- Dependencies name both sides and required date or condition.
- Critical-path and capacity calculations use consistent calendars and units.
- Risk ratings follow a defined scale; expected value is not used as a certainty.
- Personal data and staffing decisions are minimized and appropriately controlled.
- The plan includes reserves, assumptions, and change triggers.

Return the requested plan, assumptions, constraints, decisions, approvals, and first review date.
