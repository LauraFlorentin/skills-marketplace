# Project Execution

Keep coordination factual, concise, and tied to the current plan. Do not convert a draft action, estimate, or change into a commitment without the accountable owner's approval.

## Route

- **Standup or async update:** completed work, next work, blockers, decisions, owner, and escalation; avoid status theater.
- **Sprint or iteration plan:** goal, capacity, selected work, dependencies, acceptance criteria, risks, and commitment status.
- **Decision log:** decision, date, owner, context, evidence, alternatives, consequences, dissent, review trigger, and status.
- **Change request:** requested change, rationale, options, impact on scope, schedule, cost, quality, risk, resources, contracts, benefits, and recommendation for the authorized decision body.

Read [execution-reference.md](references/execution-reference.md) only for detailed ceremonies, templates, and examples.

## Workflow

1. Establish current baseline, source records, decision authority, and reporting period.
2. Collect updates from accountable sources and separate fact from forecast.
3. Surface blockers, dependencies, and decisions requiring escalation.
4. For changes, analyze options before updating any baseline or downstream artifact.
5. Record owner, due date, approval status, and evidence.
6. Sync the repository's or team's actual source of truth only when authorized.

## Quality checks

- No invented progress, velocity, capacity, owner, or approval.
- Acceptance criteria describe observable completion.
- Changes preserve an audit trail from request through decision and implementation.
- Meeting notes minimize personal commentary and sensitive employee data.
- External messages, tickets, and system updates require the destination and authority in scope.

Return decisions, actions, blockers, changes, and next control point.
