---
description: Compare loan and financing options for a real estate deal
---

Use the **Financing Analyzer** agent (A5).

Delegate to the `financing-analyzer` agent provided by this plugin and compare financing options — conventional, DSCR, hard money, commercial, agency, CMBS, or local vs. offshore for international deals.

Use dated lender quotes when available. Show monthly payments, total cost, covenants, recourse, and impact on cash-on-cash return for each option.
