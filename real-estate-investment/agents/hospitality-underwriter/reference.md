# Skill 9: Hospitality & Resort Underwriter

Analyze hotel, resort, and short-term rental investments using hospitality-specific metrics that differ fundamentally from standard rental property analysis. Hotels/resorts are **operating businesses** attached to real estate — the analysis must account for revenue volatility, seasonal demand patterns, operational complexity, and the management-intensive nature of hospitality.

## When to Use

- Deal involves a hotel, resort, boutique hotel, or aparthotel
- Property generates revenue through nightly stays (not long-term leases)
- User is evaluating a vacation rental portfolio or STR investment
- Prospectus includes ADR, RevPAR, or occupancy projections
- Property has F&B, spa, wellness, or event revenue streams
- Any deal positioned as "luxury hospitality" or "boutique accommodation"

## Hospitality-Specific Metrics

### Revenue Metrics

```
KEY REVENUE METRICS
  ADR (Average Daily Rate):
    Formula: Room Revenue ÷ Rooms Sold
    What it measures: Pricing power
    
  RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room):
    Formula: Room Revenue ÷ Total Available Room Nights
    OR: ADR × Occupancy Rate
    What it measures: Combined pricing and occupancy performance
    This is THE primary hospitality performance metric.
    
  TRevPAR (Total Revenue Per Available Room):
    Formula: Total Revenue (rooms + F&B + spa + other) ÷ Available Room Nights
    What it measures: Total revenue generation capability
    
  GOPPAR (Gross Operating Profit Per Available Room):
    Formula: GOP ÷ Available Room Nights
    What it measures: Operational profitability per room
    Best measure of management efficiency
    
  Available Room Nights:
    Formula: Total Rooms × 365 (or operating days)
    For this deal: [rooms] × 365 = [X] room nights/year
```

### ADR Validation

Always validate projected ADR against comparable properties:

```
ADR COMPARABLE ANALYSIS
  Property Name         Location     Star/Class   ADR (local)   ADR (EUR/USD)
  [Comp 1]              [area]       [class]      [amount]      [converted]
  [Comp 2]              [area]       [class]      [amount]      [converted]
  [Comp 3]              [area]       [class]      [amount]      [converted]
  
  Subject Property Projected ADR:    [amount]
  Comp Average ADR:                  [amount]
  Variance:                          +/- X%
  
  ASSESSMENT:
  - Within 10% of comps: Reasonable
  - 10-25% above comps: Aggressive — needs strong justification
  - 25%+ above comps: Unrealistic unless truly differentiated product
  - Below comps: Conservative (good) or reflects quality concerns (bad)
```

Hospitality revenues flow through more deductions than standard real estate:

  = NET REVENUE                              €___________
  
  Less: Management Fee (X% of Net Rev)       (€__________)

    Staff & Payroll                           (€__________)   ← 25-35% of revenue

    F&B Cost of Goods                         (€__________)   ← 28-35% of F&B rev
    Housekeeping & Laundry                    (€__________)
    Maintenance & Repairs                     (€__________)   ← 4-6%
    Marketing (beyond OTA)                    (€__________)   ← 3-8%
    Insurance                                 (€__________)
    Property Tax                              (€__________)
    Admin & IT                                (€__________)
    Guest Supplies & Amenities                (€__________)
    Landscaping                               (€__________)
  = GROSS OPERATING PROFIT (GOP)             €___________
  
  Less: FF&E Reserve (3-5% of revenue)       (€__________)

### OTA Commission Impact

One of the most overlooked costs in hospitality underwriting:

```
OTA (Online Travel Agency) COMMISSION ANALYSIS
  Booking Source          Commission Rate    Revenue Impact
  Booking.com             15-25%             Highest cost channel
  Expedia / Hotels.com    15-25%             Similar to Booking
  Airbnb                  3-15%              Lower but different audience
  Direct Website          0-3%               Best margin (payment processing only)
  Travel Agent            10-15%             Declining channel
  Walk-in                 0%                 Minimal for resorts
  
  CHANNEL MIX MATTERS:
    If 80% of bookings via OTAs at 20% commission:
      €1,000,000 gross → €800,000 net of commissions → 20% immediate haircut
    
    If 50% direct + 50% OTA:
      €1,000,000 gross → €900,000 net → only 10% commission drag
      
  SPONSOR'S CLAIMED CHANNEL MIX: [detail]
  REALISTIC ASSESSMENT: [detail]
  
  For new/unestablished properties:
    Year 1-2: Expect 70-90% OTA (no brand recognition yet)
    Year 3-5: Potentially 50-70% OTA with strong marketing
    Mature: 30-50% direct is achievable for strong brands
```

### Ramp-Up Period for New Properties

New hotels/resorts do NOT achieve stabilized performance immediately:

```
RAMP-UP SCHEDULE (Industry Standard)
  Year 1:    50-65% of stabilized RevPAR
  Year 2:    70-85% of stabilized RevPAR
  Year 3:    85-95% of stabilized RevPAR
  Year 4+:   Stabilized performance
  
  This means:
  - If stabilized occupancy target is 80%, expect ~50-55% in Year 1
  - If stabilized ADR target is €400, expect €300-350 in Year 1
  - Revenue in Year 1 may be 35-50% of projected stabilized revenue
  
  IMPACT ON INVESTOR RETURNS:
  Year 1 projected return (with ramp-up):    X%
  Year 1 projected return (sponsor's numbers): X%  ← likely assumes immediate stabilization
  Shortfall:                                  X%
```
