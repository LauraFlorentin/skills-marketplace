# Skill 5: Tax Strategist

Model tax benefits from real estate investments including depreciation, cost segregation, 1031 exchanges, and Real Estate Professional Status. Tax planning can add 5–15% to total returns — this skill quantifies that impact.

**Important**: Always caveat tax analysis with "consult a CPA for your specific situation." This skill provides estimates and frameworks, not tax advice.

## When to Use

- User asks "how much will I save on taxes?"
- User mentions depreciation, cost segregation, or bonus depreciation
- User asks about 1031 exchanges or tax deferral
- User wants to understand total ROI including tax benefits
- User asks about Real Estate Professional Status
- User is comparing real estate tax advantages to stocks/bonds
- After underwriting, to show the full economic picture

## Tax Benefit Calculations

### Standard Depreciation

Residential rental property depreciates over 27.5 years (straight-line). Commercial property over 39 years.

```
Depreciable Basis = Purchase Price × (1 − Land Allocation %)
  Land Allocation: typically 20% (range: 10–30% depending on location)
  
Annual Depreciation = Depreciable Basis ÷ Useful Life
  Residential: Depreciable Basis ÷ 27.5
  Commercial: Depreciable Basis ÷ 39

Annual Tax Savings = Annual Depreciation × Marginal Tax Rate

Example ($500,000 property, 20% land, 32% tax bracket):
  Depreciable Basis = $500,000 × 0.80 = $400,000
  Annual Depreciation = $400,000 ÷ 27.5 = $14,545
  Annual Tax Savings = $14,545 × 0.32 = $4,655
```

### Passive Activity Loss Rules

```
PASSIVE LOSS DEDUCTION ELIGIBILITY

1. PASSIVE INCOME OFFSET (all investors):
   Rental losses can offset other passive income (rental income from
   other properties, K-1 passive income from partnerships/syndications)
   → No income limit

2. ACTIVE PARTICIPATION EXCEPTION ($25,000):
   If investor "actively participates" (approves tenants, sets rents,
   approves repairs — not just passive ownership):
   → Deduct up to $25,000 against W-2/active income
   → Phase-out begins at $100,000 MAGI
   → Eliminated entirely at $150,000 MAGI

3. REAL ESTATE PROFESSIONAL STATUS (unlimited):
   → See REPS section below
   → No income limit on loss deductions
```

**1031 Exchange Rules Quick Reference**:
- Qualified Intermediary required (never touch the funds)
- 45-day identification period (strict, no extensions)
- 180-day closing deadline (strict, no extensions)
- Replacement property must be equal or greater value AND equal or greater debt
- 3-Property Rule: identify up to 3 properties (any value)
- 200% Rule: identify any number totaling ≤200% of sale price
- DSTs qualify as replacement property (passive, institutional-grade)
- "Swap till you drop" — continuous 1031s defer taxes until death → stepped-up basis eliminates all deferred gain

### Real Estate Professional Status (REPS)

```
REPS QUALIFICATION CHECK

Requirements (BOTH must be met):
  □ 750+ hours in real property trades or businesses annually
  □ More than 50% of total personal services in real estate

If qualified:
  → ALL rental losses become non-passive (deductible against ANY income)
  → No $25,000 limitation
  → No MAGI phase-out
  → Combined with cost segregation, can generate six-figure Year 1 deductions

REPS TAX IMPACT EXAMPLE:
  Property: $1,000,000 purchase
  Cost Seg Reclassification: 30% ($240,000 depreciable basis reclassified)
  Year 1 Bonus Depreciation: $240,000
  Additional Standard Deprec.: $20,364 (remaining $561,600 ÷ 27.5)
  Total Year 1 Depreciation: $260,364
  
  Without REPS: Passive loss (limited use against W-2 income)
  With REPS: $260,364 deduction against W-2/active income
  Tax Savings at 37%: $96,335 in Year 1 alone
```
