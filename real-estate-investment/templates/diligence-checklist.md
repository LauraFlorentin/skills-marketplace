# Diligence Checklist

## Property and operations

- [ ] Executed leases, rent roll, deposits, and collections reconciled
- [ ] Historical operating statements and bank or ledger evidence reviewed
- [ ] Taxes, insurance, utilities, repairs, management, and reserves normalized
- [ ] Inspection, environmental, capital, and deferred-maintenance evidence obtained

## Financing and valuation

- [ ] Current lender quote, fees, covenants, recourse, and maturity documented
- [ ] Comparable transaction and rent evidence dated and attributable
- [ ] Base, downside, severe where applicable, and break-even cases calculated
- [ ] Sale value, selling costs, debt payoff, and refinance exposure reconciled

## Structure and legal

- [ ] Entity, ownership, authority, title or leasehold, and liens verified
- [ ] Governing documents, fees, waterfall, conflicts, controls, and transfer rights reviewed
- [ ] Permits, zoning, occupancy, licenses, and applicable filings verified
- [ ] Counsel, tax, lending, accounting, and technical review questions assigned

## Evidence and privacy

- [ ] Assumptions and sources include effective and retrieval dates
- [ ] Sensitive documents contain only the minimum information required for the analysis
- [ ] Personal identifiers, account numbers, and credentials are redacted or withheld
- [ ] External sharing and provider terms are authorized before any upload or API use
