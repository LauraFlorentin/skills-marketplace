# Product Management Orchestrator

Route to the smallest necessary skill set and maintain one chain from evidence to decision to outcome.

## Skill registry

| Need | Skill |
|---|---|
| Direction, positioning, OKRs, competition | `strategy` |
| Research, personas, JTBD, opportunities | `discovery` |
| Roadmaps, requirements, prioritization, backlog | `planning` |
| GTM, launches, experiments, growth | `launch-growth` |
| Metrics, instrumentation, reviews | `product-analytics` |

## Routing

- Use one skill for a clearly scoped artifact.
- For a full product review: analytics → strategy → discovery gaps → planning → launch or growth actions.
- For a new opportunity: discovery → strategy choice → planning → measurement and rollout.
- For a launch: planning readiness → launch and growth → analytics, with strategy or discovery only when evidence exposes a gap.
- For a metric problem: analytics first; route to discovery when the cause needs qualitative evidence and to planning or launch-growth when a change is chosen.

Do not force every request through the full lifecycle.

## Shared controls

1. Define the user and business decision, horizon, product context, and source evidence.
2. Separate facts, customer evidence, stakeholder beliefs, model inference, and assumptions.
3. Use current sources for volatile market and competitor claims.
4. Preserve privacy, consent, accessibility, security, legal, and experiment guardrails.
5. Reconcile metric names, populations, dates, owners, and baselines across outputs.
6. Surface genuine conflicts among customer evidence, strategy, feasibility, and business constraints with options and tradeoffs; do not imply alignment.

## Synthesis

Return:

1. decision and rationale;
2. evidence quality and unknowns;
3. relevant lifecycle findings only;
4. chosen outcomes, non-goals, and tradeoffs;
5. action plan with owners and approvals;
6. measurement and review date;
7. assumptions and risks.

Do not update roadmaps, tickets, analytics systems, or stakeholder communications unless those writes are authorized by the request.
