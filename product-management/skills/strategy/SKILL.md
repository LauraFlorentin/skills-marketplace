# Product Strategy

Turn evidence and constraints into choices. Strategy artifacts are hypotheses until validated with users, stakeholders, capabilities, economics, and current market evidence.

## Intake

Establish the product, customer, problem, business model, maturity, strategic horizon, decision, constraints, existing evidence, and stakeholders. Select a lean format for early exploration and a fuller format when alignment, funding, or cross-functional execution requires it.

## Route

- **Vision:** define target user, unmet need, differentiated future state, principles, non-goals, and evidence needed.
- **OKRs:** translate strategy into a small number of outcomes and measurable results; separate outcomes from projects and guardrails.
- **Competitive analysis:** define the decision first, then compare direct, indirect, and status-quo alternatives on customer-relevant dimensions.
- **Positioning:** specify category, audience, problem, differentiated value, evidence, and alternatives.

Read [strategy-reference.md](references/strategy-reference.md) only when the task needs the detailed templates, matrices, or worked examples.

## Evidence rules

- Cite current sources for market size, competitor capabilities, prices, regulations, and trends.
- Distinguish observed fact, user input, inference, and assumption.
- Never fabricate competitor research, customer evidence, or a benchmark.
- Avoid feature-parity lists without explaining why a difference matters to the target customer.
- For OKRs, define metric owner, baseline, target, period, source, and anti-gaming guardrail.

## Workflow

1. Frame the decision and strategic tension.
2. Summarize evidence and critical unknowns.
3. Generate materially different options, including a do-nothing or narrower option.
4. Evaluate tradeoffs against explicit criteria.
5. State the chosen direction, non-goals, risks, and falsifying evidence.
6. Translate the choice into outcomes, milestones, or discovery work appropriate to the request.

## Output

Return the requested artifact plus an evidence-and-assumption register, decisions made, alternatives rejected, risks, unresolved questions, and next validation actions. Do not imply stakeholder alignment that has not occurred.
