# Launch and Growth

Launch deliberately and experiment responsibly. Adapt the workflow to product risk, market, regulation, user vulnerability, and the cost of rollback.

## Route

- **GTM plan:** audience, problem, positioning, message, channel, sales or service motion, enablement, pricing dependencies, evidence, and measures.
- **Launch:** tier, owners, readiness criteria, rollout stages, communications, support, monitoring, rollback, and post-launch review.
- **A/B test:** decision, hypothesis, unit, population, randomization, primary metric, guardrails, minimum detectable effect, duration method, exclusions, and analysis plan.
- **Growth experiment:** funnel diagnosis, intervention, evidence, cost, risk, measurement, and scale or stop rule.

Read [launch-growth-reference.md](references/launch-growth-reference.md) only for detailed templates and framework examples.

## Guardrails

- Do not expose users to unsafe, deceptive, discriminatory, or non-consensual variants.
- Coordinate privacy, security, accessibility, legal, clinical, financial, or safety review when relevant.
- Avoid repeated peeking, metric shopping, sample-ratio mismatch, interference, novelty effects, and shipping a statistically significant but immaterial result.
- Define rollback authority and operational monitoring before launch.
- Record actual current channel, market, competitor, and regulatory evidence; do not reuse stale examples.

## Workflow

1. Define the user and business decision.
2. Establish baseline, target, and constraints.
3. Choose launch or experiment scope proportionate to risk.
4. Assign owners and approval gates.
5. Instrument primary, guardrail, quality, and operational metrics.
6. Roll out in bounded stages, monitor, and stop or roll back on predefined conditions.
7. Document results, limitations, and the next decision.

Return the plan, readiness or experiment table, measurement definitions, risks, approvals, and stop or rollback criteria.
