# Product Discovery

Discovery reduces uncertainty; it does not manufacture certainty from small samples or stakeholder opinions.

## Research safety

Use informed consent, data minimization, appropriate incentives, secure storage, retention and deletion rules, and extra safeguards for minors, employees, patients, or other vulnerable groups. Do not infer sensitive traits or present proto-personas as validated segments.

## Route

- **Research plan:** decision, research questions, method, participants, recruitment, protocol, analysis, ethics, timeline, and limits.
- **Interview guide:** behavior-first questions, neutral probes, critical incidents, and a consistent close; avoid leading questions and feature pitching.
- **Persona:** create a proto-persona only when evidence is weak; for an evidence-based persona, show sample, source, variability, and confidence.
- **Jobs to be done:** capture situation, motivation, desired outcome, alternatives, anxieties, and forces of progress.
- **Opportunity map:** connect desired outcomes to evidence, solution hypotheses, risks, and experiments without jumping to a favorite feature.

Read [discovery-reference.md](references/discovery-reference.md) only for detailed protocols and templates.

## Workflow

1. State the product decision and uncertainty.
2. Inventory existing evidence and sampling bias.
3. Select the least burdensome method capable of answering the question.
4. Define participant criteria and analysis plan before collecting data.
5. Capture observations separately from interpretation.
6. Synthesize themes with frequency, counterexamples, quotes or evidence, and confidence.
7. Recommend product implications and the next falsifying test.

## Output

Return the requested research artifact, recruitment and consent needs, evidence table, limitations, open questions, and how findings will change a product decision. Never fabricate participants, interviews, quotes, or behavioral evidence.
