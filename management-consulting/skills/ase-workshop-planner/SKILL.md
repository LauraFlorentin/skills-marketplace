# ASE Workshop Planner

The Accelerated Solutions Environment (ASE) is a collaborative, multi-day event format designed to bring diverse stakeholders together to co-create and align on solutions to complex business challenges. ASE workshops address "wicked problems" by stripping away rational, emotional, and political blockers through structured creative processes.

## When to Use

- Planning a workshop for a complex, multi-stakeholder challenge
- Designing facilitation for problems that don't have an obvious answer
- Running strategic alignment sessions with executive teams
- Designing off-sites, innovation sprints, or transformation kickoffs
- Creating pre-read packages and post-workshop follow-through plans

## Key ASE Principles

- **Whole-person engagement** — address rational, emotional, and political dimensions
- **Diverge-converge cycles** — expand thinking, then focus
- **Every voice matters** — democratic participation regardless of hierarchy
- **Facilitator guides process, not content** — participants own the outcomes
- **Multi-sensory, high-energy environment** — not a standard meeting
- **Sponsor alignment before the event is critical**

## Phase 1: Pre-Workshop Design

When the user describes their workshop objective:

1. **Clarify the challenge**: Help sharpen the "wicked problem" statement. A good ASE challenge is complex, multi-stakeholder, and doesn't have an obvious answer. Use the formula: *"How might [stakeholder group] [achieve desired outcome] despite [key blocker], so that [business impact]?"*
2. **Stakeholder mapping**: Who needs to be in the room? Recommend a diverse mix (decision-makers, subject experts, frontline people, provocateurs/outsiders).
3. **Sponsor alignment**: Generate a sponsor briefing covering: objectives, success criteria, non-negotiables, expected outputs, post-event commitments.
4. **Pre-read & pre-work package**: Design what to send participants:
   - Context-setting document (the challenge, why now, what's been tried)
   - Provocative data or trend briefing
   - Personal reflection questions ("What would you do if failure wasn't an option?")
   - Logistics and what to expect (dress code, energy level, "this is not a normal meeting")

## Phase 2: Workshop Agenda Design

Build a detailed agenda based on the workshop duration (half-day, full-day, or multi-day).

### Icebreaker Pool

Select based on group size, familiarity, and energy needed:

| Icebreaker | Duration | Best For | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal Object Story | 5-10 min | < 20 people | Bring an object that represents your approach to problem-solving |
| Two Truths & a Wish | 10 min | Any size | Two truths and one thing you wish were true about the challenge |
| Speed Networking Bingo | 10-15 min | 15+ people | Find someone who... [challenge-related prompts] |
| Headline from the Future | 10 min | Any size | Write the newspaper headline you'd want to see in 2 years |
| Marshmallow Challenge | 15-20 min | Any size | Build the tallest freestanding structure (great Day 1 opener) |
| Gallery Walk Introductions | 20 min | < 25 people | Create a visual poster about yourself, then do a gallery walk |

### Energizer Pool

Deploy when energy dips — typically after lunch or during long afternoon sessions:

| Energizer | Duration | Best For | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human Knot | 5 min | < 20 people | Physical untangling exercise |
| Pattern Ball Toss | 5 min | Any size | Toss a ball in a pattern, add complexity |
| Stand If You... | 5 min | Any size | Stand if the statement applies — mix fun and topic-related |
| 60-Second Dance Party | 1-2 min | Any size | Just music and movement (requires confidence) |
| Stretch & Share | 3 min | Any size | Physical stretch combined with sharing one insight so far |
| Wrong Answer Game | 5 min | Any size | Ask simple questions, everyone must give wrong answers |

### Core Work Blocks

Structure as diverge-converge cycles:

- **Individual reflection** (5-10 min): Silent writing/thinking before any group discussion
- **Pair/trio work** (10-20 min): Build on individual ideas in small conversations
- **Small group work** (30-60 min): Deep collaborative work using:
  - Affinity mapping (clustering ideas)
  - "How Might We..." reframing
  - Dot voting for prioritization
  - Prototype/sketch solutions
  - Pre-mortem analysis ("imagine this failed — why?")
- **Plenary share-back** (15-30 min): Groups present, cross-pollinate ideas
- **Whole-room synthesis** (20-40 min): Converge on decisions, commitments, next steps

### Timing Guidelines

- Never run a work block longer than 60 minutes without a break or energizer
- Schedule energizers at: post-lunch, mid-afternoon, and any time energy visibly drops
- Multi-day: Day 1 = diverge (explore broadly), Day 2 = converge (focus on solutions)
- Buffer 10-15% of total time for overruns and spontaneous discussions
- Lunch should be at least 45 minutes — it's networking time, not wasted time

## Phase 3: Post-Workshop Package

For sustained momentum after the workshop:

1. **Within 24 hours**: Thank-you message + 3 key decisions/commitments (one-pager)
2. **Within 3 days**: Full workshop output document:
   - Photos of all artifacts (sticky notes, whiteboards, posters)
   - Synthesized key themes and decisions
   - Action items with owners and deadlines
   - Parking lot items (ideas captured but not addressed)
3. **Within 1 week**: Sponsor debrief — what worked, what to adjust, momentum plan
4. **Within 2-4 weeks**: Pulse check — are action items progressing? Need a booster session?
5. **Optional**: Short video or visual summary for broader stakeholder communication

## Operating Guidelines

- Always start by asking: What's the challenge? How many people? How much time? What's the desired output?
- Never design an agenda that's all plenary — ensure at least 40% is small group or individual work
- For groups > 30 people, always include breakout activities and limit plenary share-backs
- Icebreakers should connect to the workshop theme when possible — avoid purely silly exercises for senior executive audiences
- Flag if the objective is too broad for available time — propose scoping down or splitting into multiple sessions
- Always include energizers in any session longer than 3 hours
- If the user doesn't specify ASE format, ask if they want the ASE approach or a lighter workshop format
- For hierarchy sensitivity, ask: is the CEO in the room? Remote vs. in-person? Cultural considerations?
