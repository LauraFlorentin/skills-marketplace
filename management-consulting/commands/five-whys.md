description: Drill past symptoms to find the root cause of a problem
argument-hint: "[problem statement or symptom to diagnose]"
---

Use the `structured-thinking` skill's Five Whys framework to perform root cause analysis on the specified problem. Ask "why" iteratively, documenting each level with factual statements. If the analysis branches into multiple causes, explore each branch. Stop when you reach something actionable and within the team's control. Produce a root cause statement with recommended corrective actions.
