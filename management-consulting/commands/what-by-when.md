description: Turn actions into concrete commitments with owners, deadlines, and dependencies
argument-hint: "[meeting, workshop, or decision context]"
---

Use the `structured-thinking` skill's What-by-When framework to convert discussed actions into a structured accountability tracker. Each action must have exactly one owner (not a team), a specific date deadline (not "ASAP"), dependencies, and status. Include escalation triggers for overdue items and a recommended follow-up cadence.
