Use the **ASE Workshop Planner** skill.

Read the full skill reference at `../skills/ase-workshop-planner/SKILL.md` and help the user plan a collaborative workshop. Cover all three phases: pre-workshop design (challenge framing, stakeholder mapping, pre-read package), agenda building (icebreakers, energizers, diverge-converge work blocks), and post-workshop deliverables.

Ask the user: What's the challenge? How many people? How much time? What's the desired output?
