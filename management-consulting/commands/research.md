# Research & Analysis

> If you see unfamiliar placeholders or need to check which tools are connected, see [CONNECTORS.md](../CONNECTORS.md).

**Important**: Research findings should be verified through multiple sources before inclusion in client deliverables.

Conduct structured research for a consulting engagement.

## Usage

```
/research <type> <topic>
```

### Arguments

- `type` — Research methodology:
  - `market` — Market sizing and landscape overview
  - `competitive` — Competitive analysis and positioning
  - `benchmark` — Performance benchmarking against peers or industry standards
  - `industry` — Industry trends and dynamics analysis
  - `landscape` — Comprehensive landscape scan across players, trends, and market dynamics
- `topic` — Industry, company, market, or subject area to research

## Workflow

### 1. Define Research Questions

Clarify the specific questions the research needs to answer. Frame them as hypotheses where possible.

### 2. Gather Data

Use available tools and sources:
- Web search for public data, reports, press releases, and filings
- ~~data warehouse for internal data if connected
- ~~documents for prior research and engagement materials

### 3. Analyze and Synthesize

Apply appropriate frameworks (Porter's Five Forces, value chain analysis, TAM/SAM/SOM) and triangulate across multiple sources. Assign confidence levels to key findings.

### 4. Output

Provide:
1. Research summary with key findings
2. Supporting data and evidence
3. Confidence assessment for each major finding
4. Gaps flagged for further investigation
5. Implications for the client engagement
