description: Perform a structured RAG traffic-light assessment across multiple dimensions
argument-hint: "[project, portfolio, or initiative to assess]"
---

Use the `structured-thinking` skill's Red-Amber-Green framework to assess the specified project or portfolio across key dimensions (timeline, budget, scope, quality, resources, stakeholder satisfaction). Assign R/A/G status with brief justification for each, include trend arrows, and for any Amber or Red items, specify mitigation plans with owners and escalation paths.
