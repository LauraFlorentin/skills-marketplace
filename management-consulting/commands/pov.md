# Point of View Development

> If you see unfamiliar placeholders or need to check which tools are connected, see [CONNECTORS.md](../CONNECTORS.md).

Develop a structured point of view document that demonstrates expertise and establishes a differentiated perspective.

## Usage

```
/pov <topic>
```

### Arguments

- `topic` — The subject for the POV: an industry trend, technology shift, regulatory change, or strategic challenge

## Workflow

### 1. Research the Topic

Gather evidence, data points, and examples that support a clear thesis. Use web search for current information and ~~documents for prior firm research if connected.

### 2. Develop the Thesis

Craft a bold, specific claim — not a bland observation. The thesis should be something a reader couldn't have written themselves.

### 3. Draft the POV

Structure as:
1. **Provocative headline** — Captures the thesis in one sentence
2. **Context** — What the reader already knows (brief)
3. **The shift** — What's changing and why it matters now
4. **Implications** — Consequences for the reader's business
5. **Recommended actions** — Prescriptive, actionable steps
6. **Our perspective** — Why this firm is positioned to help

### 4. Output

Provide the complete POV draft, with guidance on tone calibration for the intended audience and distribution channel.
